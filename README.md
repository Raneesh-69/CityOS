# 🏙️ CityOS AI

An AI-powered civic management platform designed to connect citizens with government departments and streamline the reporting and resolution of everyday urban problems.

Citizens can report issues such as potholes, damaged roads, broken streetlights, garbage, and other civic problems by submitting complaints with images and details. Government officers can manage, assign, track, and resolve complaints through a centralized administrative dashboard.

## 🚀 Live Demo

**CityOS AI:** https://cityos1.netlify.app/

## Features

📸 **Citizen Issue Reporting**
Citizens can report civic problems by uploading photos and providing issue details.

🤖 **AI-Powered Issue Analysis**
AI can assist in identifying and categorizing reported civic issues from submitted information and images.

🏛️ **Department-Based Complaint Management**
Complaints can be directed to the appropriate government department for action.

👨‍💼 **Officer Management**
Administrators can assign complaints to responsible officers and track their progress.

👥 **Citizen Management**
Administrators can view and manage registered citizens through the dashboard.

📊 **Analytics Dashboard**
Provides insights into complaints, departments, issue categories, and resolution progress.

🔄 **Complaint Status Tracking**
Track complaints through different stages such as pending, assigned, in progress, and resolved.

🖥️ **Government Admin Dashboard**
A centralized interface for administrators and government officers to manage city operations.

🌐 **Full-Stack Web Application**
Designed to be deployed as a scalable web platform for modern civic administration.

## Usage

### Citizen

1. Register or log in to the CityOS platform.
2. Submit a civic complaint.
3. Upload an image of the problem.
4. Provide the location and issue description.
5. Submit the complaint.
6. Track the complaint status until resolution.

### Administrator

1. Log in to the administration dashboard.
2. View submitted complaints.
3. Manage registered citizens.
4. Manage government officers and departments.
5. Assign complaints to responsible officers.
6. Monitor complaint progress.
7. View city-level analytics and statistics.

### Government Officer

1. Log in to the officer dashboard.
2. View assigned complaints.
3. Review complaint details and uploaded images.
4. Update the complaint status.
5. Take necessary action.
6. Mark the issue as resolved after completion.
